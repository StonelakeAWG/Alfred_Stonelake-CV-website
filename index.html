Master's thesis — Excited triplet state dynamics, post electron-impact

Supervisor — Prof. Dmitry Shalashilin

Investigating the fragmentation dynamics of organofluorines — molecules central to plasma etching processes in microelectronics fabrication — on the excited triplet (T1) surface following electron-impact excitation via exchange scattering.
